# Code of conduct

## The short version

Argue about the code. Everything else is off topic.

## What that means

Everyone is welcome here, whatever they are and wherever they come from. What is
being judged is a reading, a rule, a measurement — never the person who proposed
it.

Expected:

- Say what is wrong and what would make it right. "This is wrong" is not a
  review; "this drops a row when the date column is empty, and here is the
  document" is.
- Take a correction on the argument it was made on. Being wrong in public is
  the price of working in public, and it costs nothing.
- Assume the other person read the same page you did and understood it
  differently, before assuming they did not read it.

Not accepted:

- Insults, harassment, or bringing somebody's identity into a technical
  question.
- Sexualised language or imagery, anywhere in the project.
- Publishing anybody's private information.
- Wearing somebody down: repeating a rejected argument until they stop
  answering.

## Scope

Everywhere the project happens: issues, pull requests, commits, discussions, and
any place someone is representing truecopy.

## Reporting

Email <contact@truecopy.dev>. Reports are read by the maintainer and by
nobody else, and are not repeated to the person reported.

The maintainer will edit or remove anything that breaks this page, and will say
that it was done. Someone who keeps at it stops being welcome — that decision is
the maintainer's, and it is explained when it is taken.
